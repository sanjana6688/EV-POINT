ABOUT EV POINT

EV POINT is a future-focused charging network helping drivers locate and book smart, green charging stations effortlessly. We represent clean transportation made reliable.

How It Works

Our platform aggregates real-time charging station status across the country. Users can locate nearest compatible connectors, view rates, verify port availability, and book slots instantly.

Our Vision

To establish an eco-smart charging infrastructure powered by renewable resources, supporting zero-emission electric mobility for a healthier planet.

Our Promise

A seamless charging journey—providing speed, safety, always-on customer support, and no-waiting booking convenience.
